Suggested actions

  1. Set internal expectations around the 50% range of $14M to $61M rather than the single $31M figure.
  2. Build the campaign on the horror and thriller appeal, since there is no franchise name to carry it.
  3. Use the low budget as a strength in planning, since even a moderate result would be a solid multiple of cost.
  4. Track early audience response closely and be ready to shift spend if it points toward the high or low end.
