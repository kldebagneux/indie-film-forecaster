Risks

  • The typical range is wide: half of outcomes fall between about 0.5x and 2x budget ($16.5M to $72.6M)
  • Two of the five comparables finished at roughly a third of their budget or less
  • The week of Dec 2 has 48 competing releases, so awareness could get buried

Suggested actions

  1. Build early awareness around the music and drama hook, so the film cuts through the crowded week
  2. Lean on Searchlight's Poor Things as a proof point that this kind of film can break out, and aim for the 3x scenario
  3. Check ticket presales and early buzz in the weeks before release, then shift spend toward markets that are responding
  4. Plan for a 1x outcome as the base case, with extra spend held in reserve to push if early signs point to a breakout
